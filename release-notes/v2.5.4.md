### Community models (opt-in)
• **Models → Browse → Shortlist** has a new checkbox: **Show community models (uncensored / fine-tunes)**. It's off by default; the first time you turn it on, LlamaMate explains what these models are.
• The list adds verified models from independent authors, including three with **MTP heads** (MTP is switched on automatically):
  – Qwen3.6 27B abliterated (huihui-ai) · 16.8 GB · 24 GB+ Mac · MTP
  – Qwen3.8 27B Heretic Uncensored Coder (DavidAU) · 18.5 GB · 32 GB+ Mac · MTP
  – Qwen3.6 35B A3B abliterated (huihui-ai) · 21.7 GB · 32 GB+ Mac · MTP
  – Gemma 4 12B abliterated · 7.4 GB · 16 GB+ Mac
  – Qwen3.5 4B abliterated · 2.7 GB · 8 GB+ Mac
• Community rows are marked "Community ·". Like the main list, every entry is checked against Hugging Face (exists, size, MTP heads), on each change and weekly.
